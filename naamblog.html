My Journey Learning HTML

Posted on: 23 Oct 2026 by Nida

What I Learned

I started with basic tags like h1, p and now I can build full portfolio pages with navigation and images.

Why Semantic Tags are Important?

Earlier I used only div and p tags. Now I use:

  • header for top part
  • nav for navigation
  • main for main content
  • section and article for content blocks
  • figure and figcaption for images
  • footer for bottom
